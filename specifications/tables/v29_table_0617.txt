  M                                     Mailing                                                                                 Identifies an address for mail correspondence from a
                                                                                                                                healthcare provider as stipulated by the subject.
                                                                                                                                For example, under the tenets of certain privacy regulations,

                                                                                                                                patient has a preferred address other than the one
                                                                                                                                volunteered.)

                                                                                                                                physically located and may be visited.

                                                                                                                                demographic classification or searching.  Such
                                                                                                                                addresses frequently contain insufficient information
