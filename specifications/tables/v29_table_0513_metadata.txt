  Table                                                                           0513
  Description                                                                     HL7-defined table of codes used to specify the current status of the specified
                                                                                  blood product as indicated by the placer.  For example, the placer may return the
                                                                                  blood product to the transfusion service unused because an IV could not be
                                                                                  started. The blood component may have been entered, but the line was clogged
                                                                                  and could not be used, in which case the component must be wasted. A final
                                                                                  status would indicate that the product has actually been "transfused."
