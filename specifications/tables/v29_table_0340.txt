                                                                                                                             Association’s Current Dental Terminology (CDT-2 )

                                                                                                                             genuine HCPCS codes, approved and maintained

                                                                                                                             consisting of CMS, the Health Insurance Association
                                                                                                                             of America, and the Blue Cross and Blue Shield
                                                                                                                             Association. Level III are codes developed locally
                                                                                                                             by Medicare carriers.  The HCPCS modifiers are
                                                                                                                             divided into the same three levels, I being CPT-4
                                                                                                                             modifiers, II CDT-2 and genuine HCPCS modifiers,
                                                                                                                             and III being locally agreed modifiers.

                                                                                                                             Technical Information Service (NTIS,
