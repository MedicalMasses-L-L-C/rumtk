  Table                                                                              0170
  Description                                                                        HL7-defined table of codes which specify the parents and children for
                                                                                     diagnostic studies, especially in microbiology, where the initial specimen (e.g.,
                                                                                     blood) is processed to produce results (e.g., the identity of the bacteria grown
                                                                                     out of the culture).  The process also produces new "specimens" (e.g., pure
                                                                                     culture of staphylococcus, and E. coli), and these are studied by a second order
                                                                                     process (bacterial sensitivities). The parents (e.g., blood culture) and children
                                                                                     (e.g., penicillin MIC) are identified in such cases.
