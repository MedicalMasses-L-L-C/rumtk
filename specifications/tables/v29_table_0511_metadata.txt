  Table                                                                       0511
  Description                                                                 HL7-defined table of codes used to specify the interpretation for the blood
                                                                              product observation status codes.  A status is considered preliminary until a
                                                                              blood product has reached a final disposition for the patient. For example, when
                                                                              the product is first cross-matched and a status message is sent, it would be
                                                                              considered preliminary. When the product is dispensed to the patient, that status
                                                                              would also be considered preliminary.  However, once the product is transfused,
                                                                              the status would be considered final.
